IRIS UX audit: 41 issues, 3 to fix before anything else

Two auditors reviewed the same 31 production screens independently. Their findings overlap strongly. The most serious problems are about contract data and price, not visual polish.

31 screens (14 subscription, 11 contract record, 6 back-office)6 rendered with the app's real CSS16 November 2026

How the double check was run

Same materialThe 31 Figma frames are captured web pages. Their HTML and CSS were pulled from Figma and 6 key screens were rebuilt and rendered at 1512 px. All 31 were checked from their HTML and visible text.
Two separate auditorsAuditor A (the design lead on this project) and Auditor B (a separate agent) worked from the same screens and the same 6 lenses. B never saw A's findings or opinions.
Merged afterwardsA wrote and saved its report before reading B's. Findings were then matched one by one. Where the two disagreed, both views and the decision are listed below.

Bias to keep in mind: A already knew the business rules from the redesign work (for example "pas de souscription à 70 ans ou plus"); B did not. B therefore judged copy and logic on the screen alone, which is closer to what a new counter agent sees.

    What works and should stay

    • The step 1 side panel "Bon à savoir / À préparer": it tells the agent what to collect before starting.
    • The 4-step stepper and the "Précédent / Suivant" footer: short and predictable.
    • The guarantee change tracking ("1 modifiée", "Réinitialiser").
    • The contract header on the record: status, period, premium, renewal and subscriber above the fold.
    • The back-office list pattern (KPIs, search, "Filtres avancés", table, "1 – 10 sur 19") and the production KPI band.
    • Empty states with an explanation, such as "Vous êtes à jour : aucune demande n'attend de réponse de votre part."
    • The A04 error model: "Impossible de charger les utilisateurs. Veuillez réessayer." with a "Réessayer" button.

    Limits of this audit

    • The screens come from the recette environment, so names like "test test" are test data. They still show that the forms accept incomplete adult records.
    • The captures are static. Items marked "to verify" (autosave, date logic, link targets) need a check in the live app.
    • Visual judgements cover the 6 rendered screens. The other 25 were audited for structure, copy, forms and accessibility from their HTML.
    • No user testing has been done yet. The next step is to test the fixes for the 5 priorities with 5 counter agents.
