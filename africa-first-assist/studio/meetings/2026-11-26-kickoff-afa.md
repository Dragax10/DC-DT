# 2026-11-26 · Kickoff meeting with AFA

Source: the studio lead's verbal notes after the meeting. Attendees and timings not recorded.

## What AFA asked for

**1. A B2B app (first).** For corporate clients. They should see in one place:
- their services (prestations) and their contracts;
- their beneficiaries, as a list; for a fleet client, their fleet (parc de véhicules) and what changed in it;
- all amendments (avenants) to the contract;
- the link between parent and child contracts (contrat mère / contrats filles).

**2. A B2B2C app.** For the people covered through those clients (and partners). Similar to a B2C app.

## Priority
- A clear, clean SOS flow.
- Look at what assistance apps do elsewhere. Assist America was named as a possible inspiration.

## Next mission (studio)
- UX benchmark of assistance companies' apps outside Morocco (Europe, USA): a sample of the 6 best apps.
- Collect their screens (screenshots) and any published user flows, especially SOS / assistance.
- Analyse the flows from a UX and user point of view, for inspiration.
- Constraint: we cannot buy contracts to test the apps ourselves. Sources: public screenshots, app-store listings, demo videos, help pages, Mobbin.

## Open points
- Order between B2B and B2B2C apps: B2B first, SOS flow named as the priority. To confirm how both fit in the first release.
- Who are the B2B users (fleet manager, HR, broker)? Not discussed.
- The brief (studio/brief.md) must be rewritten from these notes and signed off (gate 1).
