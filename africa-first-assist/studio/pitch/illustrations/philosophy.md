# Signal Convergence

*An algorithmic philosophy for the Africa First Assist pitch illustrations*

Signal Convergence holds that help is a field, not a place. Thousands of independent paths start scattered across the canvas, each one following a slow, layered noise current: the drift of a person who is lost, stuck or waiting. Somewhere in the field sits a single point of attraction, the tap. Its pull is weak at a distance and grows steadily as a path comes close, so that the lines that began as loose wandering bend, gather and finally run together into one luminous core. The picture is never drawn; it is the residue of the process, and every seed yields a different but equally inevitable convergence. This is a meticulously crafted algorithm, tuned through countless iterations so that chaos and pull stay in exact balance.

Movement is computed, never placed. At each step a path reads two vectors: the angle of a two-octave Perlin field and the direction of the nearest point of help. Their blend is weighted by distance, so the outer canvas stays turbulent and the inner rings grow calm and orderly. Paths are drawn with additive light, in short segments whose opacity and colour rise as they approach the core: far lines are faint, cool and thin; near lines are bright and warm with density. Where many lines agree, light accumulates on its own. The density map that emerges is the product of deep computational expertise, not decoration.

The tap itself radiates. Concentric rings grow outward from each point of help at a geometric ratio, each ring broken into dashes, as if a signal were travelling through the field. The rings are faint and exact, a counterpoint of order laid over the organic flow. Their spacing, dash rhythm and fade were refined with painstaking care so that they read as a quiet pulse rather than a target.

The system has states. With full reach, every path arrives: help is found. With reduced reach, paths stop short and hang in mid-field, never meeting the core: the hour of waiting before anyone knows where you are. With several points of help, the field divides into basins, each path choosing the nearest promise. The same algorithm tells three different stories through a single parameter change, the mark of a master-level generative system.

The algorithm also respects the page it lives on. A soft mask clears the zone where words will sit, so that the field gathers its density away from the text and the composition always leaves room to read. Every parameter, from the noise scale to the clearance curve, is exposed and reproducible: the same seed always produces the same image, and each new seed is a new print from the same plate.

## Conceptual seed

The direction of each step is pulled gently toward eight compass angles. At low strength the eye does not notice it, but over thousands of paths the field settles into the eight-fold symmetry of the khatam, the eight-pointed star of Moroccan zellige. Those who know will see it in the way the lines approach the core. Others will simply see help arriving from every direction.
