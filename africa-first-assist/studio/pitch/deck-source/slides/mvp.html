MVP · about 6 months after the brief is signed

Thirteen must-haves, built around the incident and the visa

Help in one tap

  • SOS: the call starts first, location and case follow
  • Roadside request with location
  • Case status timeline
  • Travel claim with document upload
  • Push alerts, SMS fallback

Cover in your pocket

  • Sign-in by phone code; activate by policy or partner code
  • Policy wallet, offline
  • Travel companion: numbers and steps per country, offline

Visa cover in minutes

  • Quote and buy the 5 Isaaf travel products
  • Card payment
  • Instant certificate with verification code

Foundations

  • Assistance engine with an API
  • French and Arabic (right-to-left)
  • WCAG 2.2 AA, low bandwidth, consent centre
  • AI pilots: agent copilot, cover assistant

Scope: Morocco, direct Isaaf clients and one pilot partner. Travel purchase assumes the current online subscription engine can be reused.

Should-haves if time allows: document scan prefill, vehicle profile, Wallet pass, English. WhatsApp: optional add-on.

Not in the MVP on purpose: live tracking map (needs provider GPS), teleconsultation (needs a medical partner and data-protection approval), home, funeral flow, family, human chat. Death and medical emergencies are still covered by SOS and the phone line from day one. Six months is an assumption; no deadline is known yet.
