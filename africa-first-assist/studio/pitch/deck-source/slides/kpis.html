How we will measure it

Five measures, targets set after a 90-day baseline

MeasureWhat it shows
Active usersClients active in the AFA app or a partner embed, by segment and partner
Digital case shareCases opened in the app, an embed or WhatsApp, out of all cases
Time to assistanceFrom request to provider dispatched, app versus phone
Status calls“Where is my case?” calls per 1,000 active covers
Online travel shareTravel policies sold in the app or online, out of all channels

Emergencies are never deflected away from a person. Call reduction targets apply to status and information calls only.

No baseline data yet: AFA case and call data is the first ask.

Also tracked: activation, claims with documents, time to certificate, satisfaction after each case, quality of the AI pilots, and accessibility defects at release.
