The moment that matters

A car breaks down at night near Settat. The driver calls, explains where they are, and then waits, without knowing who is coming or when.

AFA’s value is delivered in that moment. Today the only reliable channel is a phone call, with no shared location, no case status and no record in the client’s hands.

Illustrative scenario built from desk evidence. To validate in user research.

This is a hypothesis, not a user quote. The pain points come from the site review: contradicting emergency numbers, a free-text claim form with no upload and no tracking. User research after kickoff will test it.
