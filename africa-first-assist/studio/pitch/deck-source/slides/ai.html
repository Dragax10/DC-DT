V2

Guided declaration, Darija voice

Speak or add photos; the case is filled in for the agent. Darija voice as a measured pilot.

Human check: agent confirms before dispatch or payment.

Not sold as AI: the passport scan and Schengen check (OCR and rules) and travel alerts (rules and a data feed). Health data and AI hosting need data-protection review, to confirm by legal counsel.

Benchmark: Lemonade, Ping An, Discovery, AtlantaSanad all keep a human in the loop.

Why the copilot first: it serves every case on every channel, including partner beneficiaries who never install the app, and it carries the lowest consumer risk because a person decides. Darija voice would be unique in the benchmark but carries the highest delivery risk, so it is a V2 pilot with human review. Main constraint for all AI: where data is processed.
