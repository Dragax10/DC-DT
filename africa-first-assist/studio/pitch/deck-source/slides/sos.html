Key flow · SOS

The call always comes first

1

Tap SOS

On every screen, even locked or signed out.

2

One confirm

Cancel window; no countdown with a screen reader. Options: “for someone else”, “a relative has died”.

3

Call starts

A person answers first: no recording or menu before them.

4

In the background

Location, policy and case sent; retries never delay the call.

5

Case and SMS

Agent matches the case; client sees status and gets an SMS.

If the call fails: retry, request a callback, or use local emergency numbers saved offline.

Bereavement: straight to the funeral team. No ratings, surveys or marketing.

UX flow U02 on the FigJam board. Phone behaviour to confirm with the build team.

Redrawn after our internal review: GPS, network and SMS never block the call. Guests see a 4-digit code before the call so the agent can match the case. Help for someone else: the person in trouble can share their location through a link.
