One engine, two ways in

Way in 1 · MVP

AFA app

Direct Isaaf clients, and partners who opt in with a co-branded experience.

Core · MVP

Assistance engine with an API

Cases, SOS, status, cover lookup, copilot. One source of truth.

Way in 2 · V2 pilot, V3 scale

Partner apps

SOS, request and status embedded in partner insurers’ and banks’ apps, under the partner’s brand.

  • No competition with partner apps: partners choose co-branding or an embed.
  • Phone clients get an SMS status link, so even non-installers see their case.

Alternatives considered: AFA app only; partner embeds only.

Why not AFA app only: most beneficiaries belong to partner contracts, and partners already run apps; an AFA app alone risks competing with its own clients. Why not partner embeds only: AFA stays invisible and has no direct retail. The hybrid needs an API-first engine from day one, which is why it is in the MVP. To test in 2–3 partner interviews.
