Questions for AFA teams

What we need to know to design it well

TeamOur questions
Business and productWho are the clients, by group? Which markets and languages first?
PartnersCan AFA invite partners’ clients to the app? How should brands appear?
ITWhat cover and request information can the app show? What exists online today?
Legal and complianceWhich consents and wording are needed for sales, claims and AI?
OperationsHow are requests handled today? Can we spend time with agents?
Medical teamWhat medical help could the app offer later?

Each answer belongs to that team; we design around it.

These are questions, not recommendations. Each answer changes what we design, so we ask them early, in the discovery phase.
