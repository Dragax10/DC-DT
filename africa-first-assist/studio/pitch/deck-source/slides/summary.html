Summary

An experience built around the moment people need help

What we learned

Help today means a phone call

People explain where they are, then wait without knowing who is coming. No Moroccan assistance company offers a consumer app.

What we propose

One tap to help, status to follow

SOS that starts the call, a request status to follow, cover always at hand, and travel cover with an instant certificate. In French and Arabic, accessible to all.

How we get it right

Research, test, then design

Interviews with clients and agents, usability tests of the flows, then screens. Each AFA team keeps its own decisions.

Desk research and a review of 41 apps, November 2026. To validate with AFA and its clients.

Three messages. One: the moment that matters is an incident, and today it runs on the phone. Two: an experience designed around that moment and around AFA’s travel product. Three: we validate everything with real clients before designing screens.
