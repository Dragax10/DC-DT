10 questions

What we need from you to start

  1. Why an app, and why now? What triggered it?
  2. In two years, what has changed if it works? Which 2–3 goals count most?
  3. Who is it for first: your direct clients, partners’ clients, or both?
  4. Should it sell (for example travel cover), or only serve people already covered?
  5. How does a request for help work today, and where does it hurt most?
  1. Which figures can you share as a baseline: calls, delays, complaints, online sales?
  2. Your partners have their own apps. Should AFA’s app complement them or stand alone?
  3. What is already decided: date, budget, tech partner, group standards?
  4. Who decides and signs off, and who is our day-to-day contact?
  5. Can we talk to clients and agents in the coming weeks?

Technical, legal and budget questions go to the AFA teams concerned. We note answers; we don’t decide them.
