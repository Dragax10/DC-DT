Backup · Experience principles

Seven rules every screen follows

1 The call comes first

Nothing delays reaching a person in an emergency.

2 A person is one tap away

On every screen, including the AI assistant.

3 Show the status

Nobody should have to call to ask where help is.

4 Plain words, French and Arabic

Right-to-left done properly; no insurance jargon.

5 Works on weak connections

Cover card and emergency numbers work offline.

6 Accessible to everyone

WCAG 2.2 AA: large targets, screen readers, text size.

7 Respect hard moments

No marketing or ratings after a death or a refusal.

Principles to test and refine with users.

These principles turn into acceptance criteria for every screen and for usability tests.
