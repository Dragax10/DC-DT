How we work with AFA

Research, test, then design

1

Discover

4–6 weeks. Interviews with clients, partners’ clients and agents. Review of call reasons and feedback.

AFA signs the brief

2

Define

Validated journeys and UX flows. Wireframes. Two or three design directions.

AFA picks a direction

3

Prototype and test

Clickable prototype. Usability tests in French and Arabic, including accessibility.

Findings shared

4

Design and hand-off

Final screens, design system and specifications for the build team.

AFA approves

Build planning, cost and technical choices are made by AFA IT and its tech partner; we work alongside them.

Durations are indicative; to agree with AFA.

Each phase ends with an AFA sign-off. Nothing goes to clients or developers without AFA’s approval.
