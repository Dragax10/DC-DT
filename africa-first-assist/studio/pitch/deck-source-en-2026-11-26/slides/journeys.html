Four journeys shape the MVP

Visa applicant

Needs a certificate the consulate accepts, today

Today: “from” prices, separate site, agency queue.

MVP: buy, scan passport, certificate in minutes.

Family abroad

Needs help in a foreign city, without roaming

Today: phone only; PDFs; mixed currencies.

MVP: offline wallet, local numbers, SOS, claim upload.

Partner driver

Broken down; may not know the AFA name

Today: calls, explains location, waits blind.

First version: phone + SMS link to the request status; app if AFA invites them.

Health plan member

Needs medical help at night

Today: phone; no digital health service.

MVP: SOS and cover. V2: teleconsultation.

Built from desk evidence, not interviews. A fifth journey, a death abroad, is handled by people first; see appendix.

Full journey maps and service blueprint in the appendix.

These are proto-personas. Emotions and needs are hypotheses until we interview real clients. The partner driver is the key one for scale: most will use the phone, so the backstage must work without the app.
