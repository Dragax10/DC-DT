## VERDICT: Not ready

The research is careful and the AI guardrails are strong. Three things block the deck:
1. The main differentiator ("no Moroccan app offers geolocated SOS / teleconsultation") is contradicted by our own files.
2. The SOS flow, as drawn, makes the call wait for GPS, network calls and an SMS step. That breaks our own rule that SOS is never blocked.
3. The deck promises 1M+ users, but nothing in the MVP or V2 shows how to get there. Cost, team, timeline and KPI targets are also missing.

All three can be fixed in a day or two. No new research is needed.

## WHAT WORKS (keep these)

1. **Honest evidence labels everywhere.** (A), (Q), (H), "snippet", "not found ≠ absent", and the proto-persona warning are applied throughout. This is the package's best defence in the room. Keep the labels on the slides.
2. **The human-in-the-loop design is unusually solid.** F07's "never do" table, HITL-1 to HITL-6 in JM §7.3, the rules-only cover pre-checks in which "the app never refuses", the human-only funeral route, and "no free-generated text sent without approval" all match decisions.md's lower-risk AI lead. Use this as the AI slide.
3. **The shared edge-state system and the RTL rules.** E-NET, E-GPS, E-OTP, E-PAY, E-POL, E-COV and E-HRS plus bidi isolation (numbers, plates, passport names in Latin script) give the flows real completeness. The insight that P3 may never install the app, so phone + SMS link + copilot must work without it, is the strongest strategic point and supports model C.

## Deck vs appendix

**Must be in the deck (about 12–15 slides)**
1. Executive summary: the recommendation (model C, MVP scope, AI lead), why, and what we need from AFA.
2. Situation: AFA is B2B2C, delivers under partner brands, has no app, and the market is moving (insurer apps, Wafa IMA Sérélia, AtlantaSanad Assia). Neutral wording, no "lost position" (finding 6).
3. The moment that matters: the first minutes after an incident (JM summary 2), as one illustrative journey (P3), labelled hypothesis.
4. The differentiator, correctly worded (finding 1).
5. Distribution model C, and the "Path to 1M+" funnel (finding 3).
6. MVP: one canonical feature list (finding 10), with the three promises.
7. AI in the workflow: copilot + FR/AR cover assistant, with human-in-the-loop points (What works 2). Darija voice shown as a V2 pilot.
8. SOS flow (the redrawn version, finding 2) and the service blueprint in a simplified form.
9. Roadmap MVP → V2 → V3, with prerequisites.
10. Regulatory and data points to confirm (finding 7), framed as a joint workstream.
11. KPIs (headline 4–5), and "baseline first".
12. Cost, team and timeline ranges (finding 3).
13. The ask / next steps: data request, partner-rights answer, Gate 1 brief, pilot partner.

**Appendix only**
- Full benchmark tables and matrices (BM §2–3); the full RICE table (RM §4.2).
- The full feature catalogue (RM §2) and the AI table (RM §3.2).
- All five proto-personas and journey maps (P1, P2, P4, P5). P5 should not appear as a main-deck story.
- Recognition source list, ownership and leadership details, the market-share tables.
- The flow files 00–09 (show one or two simplified flows in the deck, not the mermaid diagrams).
- The research plan (JM §8) and the ranked assumptions (JM §9).
