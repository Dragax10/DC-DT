# BENCHMARK: insurance and assistance mobile apps (national, Africa, international)

Date: 2026-11-18
Author: research
Type: **DESK RESEARCH. Not user research.** Everything here comes from web search result snippets (press, company pages, third-party listings). It says what companies *claim* their apps do. It does not say what users want, use or value. Do not cite it as evidence of user behaviour.
Purpose: give AFA's pitch package a sourced list of what comparable apps offer, with AI features flagged. Context: decision log 2026-11-18 (scope = mobile app for AFA clients, AI built in, target 1M+ clients).

## How this was done, and its limits
- About 45 web searches on 2026-11-18. Feature claims come from the search engine's snippets and summaries of the linked pages.
- **App stores could not be opened.** `apps.apple.com` and app-data sites (`mwm.ai`) did not resolve from this session. So **no store ratings, download counts or "last updated" dates were checked.** Store URLs below are cited only when the search snippet quoted the listing text.
- Many sources are press releases or trade press from 2015 to 2026. Features change. The date column shows the date of the source where visible.
- Markers used:
  - **✓** feature stated by a company page, app-store listing text, or press coverage of a launch.
  - **(✓)** stated only by a secondary or weak source (comparison site, blog), or by an older/legacy version of the app. Verify before reuse.
  - Blank = not found. **Blank does not mean the app lacks the feature.** It means no source was found.
- "AI" is only flagged when a source says AI, machine learning, computer vision, LLM or an equivalent. Automation that is not described as AI is labelled "automation".

## 1. Executive summary

1. **Table stakes everywhere:** view policy / digital card, declare a claim, track a claim, find an agency/garage/clinic, and one-tap call to assistance. Every group has these. An AFA app without them would look behind the market.
2. **Moroccan apps are insurer self-service, not assistance apps.** My Wafa, MyRMA, MyAXA Maroc, Sanlam Maroc and AtlantaSanad focus on motor and health contracts, payment, quotes and claims. **No Moroccan app found offers geolocated SOS, live tow-truck tracking, teleconsultation or travel assistance features.** That is the open space for AFA. The nearest local move is Wafa IMA Assistance's "Sérélia" senior teleassistance via a connected watch (Feb 2026), a direct assistance competitor.
3. **Messaging is a real channel in Morocco and Africa, often ahead of the app.** AtlantaSanad (WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram chatbot, then AI assistant "Assia" in Sept 2025), MCMA M3ak (WhatsApp motor service, 2023), Old Mutual and Hollard (WhatsApp claims), Turaco (claims mostly on WhatsApp), AXA Mansard ("Karis" WhatsApp bot).
4. **Inclusion patterns in Africa:** USSD on 2G with no data (BIMA, AXA Mansard x Airtel, Old Mutual), premiums through mobile-operator billing or wallets (BIMA, PalmPay), mobile-money payouts (Turaco), SMS fallback for critical alerts (Chubb Travel Smart).
5. **International assistance apps converge on a "travel companion" pattern:** location-based safety alerts, local emergency numbers, one-tap call or chat with the assistance centre, nearby facility finder, teleconsultation, translation help, and claims with photo upload (Allianz TravelSmart, Allyz, AXA My Trip Companion, International SOS, Chubb, Healix).
6. **Roadside done digitally:** Europ Assistance shows incident geolocation and real-time tow-truck tracking; MAPFRE GO requests tow truck, ambulance, locksmith or plumber in one click. Not found in any Moroccan app.
7. **AI trends, by frequency in this sample:** (a) AI symptom checkers / AI doctors (Discovery, Allyz with Infermedica, Ping An AI Doctor, Oscar "Oswell"); (b) photo-based damage assessment (Ping An about 3 minutes, Hollard with Bdeo "within 15 minutes", Genertel); (c) conversational claims and service bots (Lemonade AI Jim, Zurich WhatsApp claims, AtlantaSanad Assia); (d) fraud scoring (Turaco, Lemonade, Zurich); (e) parametric or automated payouts (Allianz flight-delay payments, Pula satellite-based claims).
8. **AI always has a human backstop in the sources:** Ping An "AI responses in seconds + full-process supervision by human doctors"; Discovery says its bot does not give medical advice; Lemonade's AI Jim pays or hands over to a human.
9. **Scale proof points for a 1M+ target:** Ping An AI Doctor 90M monthly active users (2026 release); BIMA 2M tele-doctor consultations; Pula 4.5M farmers; Namola 500k+ sign-ups. These are company figures.
10. **Flag for a human decision:** several African sources suggest clients prefer WhatsApp/USSD for claims (Turaco CEO: "that's what people like to use to communicate"). The current scope is an app. Whether AFA's app should be paired with a WhatsApp/USSD channel is a scope question for the studio lead and client, and needs user research in Morocco to answer.

## 4. Patterns

### 4a. Table stakes (found across all three groups)
- See your policy or a digital card (N1, N4, N6, A3, A5, A6, I1, I7, I14).
- Declare a claim and follow it (N1, N4, N7, A3, A13, I1, I7, I16, I17).
- Find a nearby agency, garage, clinic or pharmacy (N1, N5, N7, A6, I1, I4).
- Reach the company fast: one-tap call or chat (N5, I1, I8, I15).
- Pay online (N1, N4, I14, I16).

### 4b. Differentiators (found in some apps, rare in Morocco)
- **Assistance-first features:** geolocated SOS (A2, A11, A12, I4, I8); roadside request with live tow-truck tracking (I7; legacy SMS version I16); home assistance on demand (I7, I16, N6 home care).
- **Travel companion:** safety and flight alerts, emergency numbers, facility finder, translation (I1, I2, I4, I8, I9, I10). Visa help (I3, I5, I15). AFA sells travel and Schengen visa products today, so this group is the closest model.
- **Health in the app:** teleconsultation with e-prescription and medicine delivery (A1, A7, I3, I14).
- **Family:** act for a relative (A12). Rare in this sample.
- **Wellness and behaviour rewards:** Vitality, driving score rewards (A1, A2).

### 4d. What looks relevant for Morocco and Africa (hypotheses to test, not findings about users)
- **Channel:** Moroccan insurers already run WhatsApp services (N7, N8). African players run claims on WhatsApp (A3, A5, A9). An AFA app may need a WhatsApp companion for SOS intake and claim documents. Needs research with AFA clients.
- **Bandwidth:** USSD (A3, A7, A8, A10) and SMS fallback for critical alerts (I9) are proven patterns where data is costly or unreliable. Relevant for AFA's ~20 African countries and for travellers abroad without roaming data.
- **Payment:** Moroccan apps show card/online payment (N1, N4). Mobile money and operator billing are proven elsewhere in Africa (A7, A8, A9). No Moroccan mobile-money example was found in this search; needs a separate check of Moroccan wallets.
- **Language:** only MaCNSS (N10) shows an Arabic interface. No Darija voice or text support was found anywhere. Arabic, French and Darija support is a possible differentiator; not validated.
- **AFA's own products map to proven patterns:** Isaaf Visa Europe → AXA Schengen digital card and visa help (I5, I3); Isaaf Auto → Europ Assistance digital roadside with tow-truck tracking (I7); Assistance Médicale → teleconsultation + e-prescription (A1, I3); Assistance Domicile → one-tap home help (I7, I16); Assistance Décès → digital funeral claims with fast payout (A3, A4).
- **Local competitor to watch:** Wafa IMA Assistance (N3) is the only Moroccan assistance company found making a digital assistance move (Sérélia, Feb 2026).
- **Group assets:** SanlamAllianz already ships client apps in Cameroon (MySAllia) and a motor-claims app in Madagascar (M'LAM). Worth asking the client whether AFA should reuse group platforms.

## 5. Gaps and caveats

**Could not verify**
- **All ratings, download counts and update dates.** App stores and app-data sites were unreachable from this session. To fill this, `apps.apple.com` and `play.google.com` would need to be allowed in the session network settings, or a person checks the stores manually.
- **ER24** app: no ER24-specific source found. Not listed.
- **World Nomads, Battleface:** no app with described features found (assistance and claims are described as phone/web services). Not listed.
- **Mondial Assistance:** treated as part of Allianz Partners (I1, I2). No separate "Mondial" app found.
- **Allianz Maroc client app:** not found. Post-merger SanlamAllianz Maroc app plans: not found.
- **Moroccan bancassurance apps** (e.g. bank apps selling insurance): not searched in depth. Gap.
- **Wafa IMA Assistance** standalone app: not found; only the digital platform and Sérélia.
- **MAMDA chatbot** (Mar 2023): headline only, content not seen.
- **Ping An "photo claims in minutes" figures, Lemonade "2 seconds", Turaco "4 hours", Sanlam "4 business hours"**: company claims, often single cases or older releases.

**Source quality**
- Many Moroccan feature lists come from 2017–2021 press or from wafir.ma (a comparison site with visible inconsistencies). These are marked (✓).
- Store listing text was used only when quoted in a search snippet.
- Several apps vary by country (Europ Assistance, Allianz, AXA, Old Mutual, Hollard, MAPFRE). The country shown is the one the source describes.

**What would close the gaps**
1. Store check (iOS and Play, Morocco storefront) for N1, N4, N5, N6, N7: ratings, review themes, last update, languages. Half a day.
2. Hands-on teardown of the 5 Moroccan apps and 3 assistance leaders (I1/I2, I7, I8): screenshots of onboarding, SOS, claims. One to two days.
3. User research with AFA clients (travel, motor, medical) on channel preference (app vs WhatsApp vs phone), language, payment, and what they need in the first hour of an incident. This benchmark cannot answer those questions.
