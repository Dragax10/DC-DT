## Style (préfixe commun à chaque prompt)
Storyboard frame, Notion-style illustration: simple hand-drawn black ink line art on a pure white background, no color, no grey fills, minimal details, slightly playful rounded characters with simple faces, clean confident outlines, lots of white space, no text, no letters.
Personnage fixe J1 : « a man in his late thirties with short dark hair and a casual jacket ».
