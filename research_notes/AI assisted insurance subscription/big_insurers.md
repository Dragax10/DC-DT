# How large global insurers and travel/assistance insurers use AI and agentic AI in distribution, sales and underwriting (quote-to-bind)

Method note: these notes come from 14 web searches (October 2026). WebFetch could not reach any of the pages (DNS errors on insurancebusinessmag.com, zurichna.com, insuranceerm.com and actuary.info), so the figures below are taken from search-result summaries of the cited pages and not from full-text reads. Before quoting a number in the final report, check it against the source page. Most metrics are company-reported and unaudited.

## Q1. What have the major insurers deployed for sales/agent assistance and underwriting with AI and generative/agentic AI (2022–2026)?

### Takeaway
The best-documented deployments help underwriters and contact-centre staff. They summarise documents, gather information and answer knowledge queries, and a human still decides. AI that sells and binds policies on its own is rare. The clearest cases are Allstate (AI closing policies without an agent in 3 US states, which the CEO called "a learning") and Ping An (an "AI salesforce" with reported sales of about RMB140bn in 2024). Most European majors (Allianz, AXA, Generali) report productivity platforms and use-case counts, not sales outcomes.

### Cited Findings
**Allianz**
- BRIAN (Allianz UK commercial lines): a gen-AI "brain" for underwriters. It went live in January 2025 after a pilot and has handled more than 13,000 underwriter queries. Allianz estimates it saved about 135 working days of information gathering. It is an internal assistant: underwriters ask questions and it retrieves guidance. It does not bind policies. — [Allianz UK](https://www.allianz.co.uk/news-and-insight/news/brian-the-ai-brain.html); [InsurTech Digital](https://insurtechdigital.com/news/allianz-what-ai-can-do-to-give-underwriters-a-headstart)
- Project Nemo (Allianz Australia): 7 specialised agents handle food-spoilage claims under AUD 500, with a reported ~80% cut in claim time. This is claims, not sales, but it is Allianz's flagship multi-agent example. — reported in search summary of [Allianz Annual Report 2025](https://www.allianz.com/content/dam/onemarketing/azcom/Allianz_com/investor-relations/en/results-reports/annual-report/ar-2025/en-allianz-se-annual-report-2025.pdf) / trade press (verify)
- Evident's AI Insurance Index (June 2025) ranked Allianz and AXA at the top. Evident also noted that "Revenue-generating AI is yet to appear on our outside-in assessment." — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/06/24/insurance-companies-ai-ranking-allianz-axa-evident-insights-eye-on-ai/); [Insurance Business](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/technology/allianz-leads-the-pack-as-insurers-race-to-turn-ai-investment-into-real-competitive-advantage-579109.aspx)
- Flag: DigitalDefynd claims a "30% reduction in policy approval time" for Allianz underwriting but gives no primary source. Do not rely on it. — [DigitalDefynd](https://digitaldefynd.com/IQ/allianz-group-using-ai/)

**AXA**
- AXA Secure GPT: an internal gen-AI platform built with Microsoft on Azure OpenAI Service in about 3 months. It was piloted with 1,000 employees at AXA Group Operations, with a target of all ~140,000 staff (2023). — [AXA press release](https://www.axa.com/en/press/press-releases/axa-offers-securegenerative-ai-to-employees); [Microsoft customer story](https://www.microsoft.com/en/customers/story/1760377839901581759-axa-gie-azure-insurance-en-france) (vendor case study)
- The 2023 Integrated Report says Secure GPT was already used by 105,000 employees. It also states a plan to invest in the agent network with AI to reduce agents' administrative work and free time for sales. That is a stated intent, not a measured result. — [AXA Integrated Report 2023](https://www-axa-com.cdn.axa-contento-118412.eu/www-axa-com/8aa103fe-d0aa-40fb-9dd4-6f7de7712df8_axa_ri2023_accessibleb_va.pdf)
- A secondary source says AXA has about 400 predictive, generative and agentic AI use cases, with no breakdown for distribution. — [CIO Dive](https://www.ciodive.com/news/insurance-industry-generative-ai-adoption-evident-manulife-axa/751874/)
- I found no published AXA metric for a sales or agent copilot.

**Zurich**
- US Middle Market: Sixfold's gen-AI underwriting tool was used on 80% of submissions handled by 16 underwriters, saving an estimated average of 60 minutes per submission in the initial rollout. Sixfold had processed more than 1 million submissions across 40+ lines by December 2025. Underwriters review and decide. — [Zurich North America](https://www.zurichna.com/media/news-releases/2025/ai-tool-brings-real-world-value-to-zurich-usmm-underwriters)
- UK commercial: an AI underwriting workbench cut processing time by 78% in under two years. Zurich links the gain to faster response to brokers. — [Insurance Post](https://www.postonline.co.uk/technology/7960848/zurich-cuts-commercial-underwriting-time-by-78-with-ai); [AI at Zurich](https://www.zurich.com/about-us/ai-at-zurich)

**Generali**
- Only aggregate figures turned up, such as more than €200M in operating cost savings from AI across claims and underwriting. I found no assistant-level metric. — [contextwindows.ai case study](https://www.contextwindows.ai/case-study/generali-global-corporate-commercial-underwriting-automation) (third-party, verify)

**Aviva**
- A gen-AI tool summarises GP medical reports for life underwriting. Aviva says it roughly halves the time underwriters spend reviewing each case. It was announced in November 2025 and extended to Critical Illness in March 2026. Underwriters still make the decision. — [Aviva Nov 2025](https://www.aviva.com/newsroom/news-releases/2025/11/aviva-to-launch-groundbreaking-ai-underwriting-tool/); [Aviva Mar 2026](https://www.aviva.com/newsroom/news-releases/2026/03/aviva-extends-industry-first-ai-underwriting-tool-to-critical-illness-cover/). A third-party source gives the ~50% figure as a testing-phase result.

**Ping An**
- 2024: AI service representatives handled about 1.84 billion interactions, 80% of total customer-service volume. The results deck reports "Sales realized by AI agents: ~RMB140bn". — [Ping An 2024 annual results presentation](https://group.pingan.com/resource/pingan/IR-Docs/2025/pingan-ar24-presentation.pdf); [Ping An 2024 press release](https://group.pingan.com/media/news/2025/pingan-ar24-press-release.html)
- 2025: about 1,702 million AI service interactions (still ~80% of volume) and AI-agent sales of RMB133.18bn, slightly down on 2024. — [Ping An 2025 results](https://group.pingan.com/media/news/2026/ar-25.html). See also [InsuranceERM](https://www.insuranceerm.com/news-comment/ping-an-reveals-sales-figures-for-its-ai-salesforce.html) and [Insurance Journal, Mar 2026](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/international/2026/03/31/864014.htm)
- The CTO describes a shift to "AI agent-centric" services. — [Yicai Global](https://www.yicaiglobal.com/news/chinas-ping-an-insurance-shifts-to-ai-agent-centric-services-cto-says)
- Caveat: Ping An does not clearly say whether "sales realized by AI agents" means sales closed autonomously or sales where AI took part (for example, outbound AI calls followed by human closing).

**Chubb**
- Chubb Studio (embedded distribution platform) added an AI optimisation engine. It offers persona-matched recommendations, "click-to-engage" hand-off to human advisors by phone, video or text, and real-time campaign analytics. More than 250 partners use the platform. — [Reinsurance News](https://www.reinsurancene.ws/chubb-unveils-new-ai-powered-embedded-insurance-optimisation-engine/); [Beinsure](https://beinsure.com/news/chubb-launches-ai-optimisation-engine/)
- Chubb Digital premium grew 27% in 2025 to $1.4bn, with an underwriting profit. The CEO has signalled a roughly 20% headcount cut over 3–4 years from AI and digitisation, worth about 1.5 combined-ratio points. — [Insurance Business](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/breaking-news/chubb-ceo-signals-significant-workforce-reductions-as-ai-strategy-accelerates-568927.aspx); [Coverager](https://coverager.com/chubb-lays-out-digital-transformation-plan/)

**Allstate**
- On the Q1 2026 earnings call, CEO Tom Wilson said Allstate's AI is selling policies without an agent in 3 states, calling it "more of a learning". The capability sits in ALLIE, the internal AI platform, which drafts more than 50,000 claims messages a day. A "customer engagement sidekick" assists human agents during calls. — [actuary.info analysis](https://actuary.info/insights/allstate-ai-direct-sales-closing-policies-distribution-2026) (secondary; check the earnings transcript)

**State Farm**
- Contact-centre staff reportedly have an AI knowledge assistant that surfaces answers from internal knowledge bases. It is not a customer-facing bot. — [Perspective AI blog](https://getperspective.ai/blog/state-farm-s-ai-roadmap-how-the-largest-us-insurer-is-modernizing-customer-experience-in-2026) (vendor blog, low reliability)
- State Farm joined OpenAI's "Frontier" programme. — [actuary.info](https://actuary.info/insights/state-farm-openai-frontier-96m-policies-ai) (secondary)
- The 2026 agent-contract changes, including mandated AI use, are covered under Q3. — [Carrier Management, May 2026](https://www.carriermanagement.com/news/2026/05/12/287814.htm)

**Progressive**
- On the Q4 2025 call, the CEO said some direct-channel sales "might be through an agentic AI agent" and that the direct channel could change dramatically. She also said independent agents remain important for complex "knee-to-knee" needs. — reported via [Perspective AI](https://getperspective.ai/blog/ai-insurance-customer-service-2026-state-of-conversational-carriers) (secondary; check the transcript)

**Tokio Marine**
- September 2025 strategic collaboration with OpenAI: AI agents built with deep-research capabilities will help branch offices with sales decisions and customer-inquiry responses. — [Tokio Marine release](https://www.tokiomarinehd.com/en/newsroom/release/ikr22500000008w0-att/20250925_OpenAI_e.pdf)
- August 2025 Salesforce partnership: Agentforce will be deployed in contact centres, agencies and branches, covering "policy enrollment through after-sales". — [Tokio Marine release](https://www.tokiomarinehd.com/en/newsroom/release/o1ckc9000001ymth-att/20250808_Salesforce_e.pdf)
- Shift Technology gen-AI visual intelligence is used for claims and fraud (not sales). — [Shift Technology](https://www.shift-technology.com/resources/press/tokio-marine-deploys-shift-technologys-gen-ai-for-claims-fraud-detection) (vendor press release)
- No outcome metrics were published.

### Inferences
- Autonomy runs along a clear range. Most deployments only suggest (summarise, retrieve, recommend) and a human underwriter or agent decides. Hand-off designs (Chubb click-to-engage) sit in the middle. True autonomous selling is limited to simple personal lines (Allstate auto/home in 3 states) and Ping An's high-volume Chinese channel.
- Published time savings cluster in underwriting document work, at roughly 50–78% per case or about 60 min per submission. Conversion uplift figures from named insurers are almost absent.

### Gaps
- MetLife, Munich Re/ERGO and Generali: I found no source with a specific sales or agent-assist deployment and metrics. Searches returned nothing.
- I found no AXA or Allianz agent/broker copilot with published conversion or handling-time metrics.
- I could not confirm the exact wording of the Allstate and Progressive earnings-call statements from transcripts, because WebFetch failed.

## Q2. What do Europ Assistance, AXA Assistance/Partners, Allianz Partners and other travel/assistance players do with AI in travel insurance sales?

### Takeaway
Public evidence of AI in travel insurance *sales* is thin. Assistance players mostly report AI in claims and service. Sales-side innovation is in embedded and API distribution (AXA Partners, Chubb Studio), and the few chat channels used for sales are still staffed by humans.

### Cited Findings
- Allianz Partners (US): AI cut the average claim lifecycle from 19 days to 4, with 71% processed in 12 hours or less and payments as fast as 6 hours. AI reviews most claims and recommends them for approval. AI also powers chat on the consumer website for inquiries. Quoting remains a standard web form (plan type, dates, ages, trip cost, state). — [Travel Weekly](https://www.travelweekly.com/Travel-News/Travel-Agent-Issues/Allianz-Partners-leverages-AI-for-faster-payouts); [Allianz Travel Insurance](https://www.allianztravelinsurance.com/find-a-plan)
- Allianz Partners also launched a universal claims portal with AI capabilities. — [TravelPulse](https://www.travelpulse.com/news/travel-insurance/allianz-partners-launches-new-universal-claims-portal-with-ai-capabilities)
- Allianz Partners and HCF (Australia): instant online chat for travel insurance customers. At launch all chats were handled by live human agents, with "AI capabilities to be introduced over the coming weeks". The products are embedded in partners' businesses. — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/allianz-partners-introduces-instant-online-chat-for-hcf-travel-insurance-customers-302113945.html)
- AXA Partners: a travel API is listed as usable by AI agents to quote, sell and service policies from partner checkout and booking flows. This is a third-party API-catalog listing, not an AXA announcement. — [Jentic listing](https://jentic.com/apis/axa-assistance.com/axa-assistance)
- AXA Partners and bolttech (October 2025): a partnership for embedded insurance and assistance across the EU, UK and Switzerland. — [PR Newswire](https://tools.prnewswire.com/en-us/live/20823/release/20251015EN97141)
- An older AXA chatbot case (innoq) covered customer service, not sales, and is pre-2022 or undated. — [innoq](http://www.innoq.com/en/cases/axa-chatbot-als-unterstuetzung-im-kundenservice/)
- Europ Assistance: a TCS deal to rework its IT operating model with AI/ML, including co-innovation on gen-AI use cases. No sales metrics. — [TCS](https://www.tcs.com/who-we-are/newsroom/press-release/tcs-transform-europ-assistance-it-operating-model-using-artificial-intelligence-machine-learning)
- Generali rebranded its care platform (Europ Assistance plus Generali Employee Benefits) as "Redion", which calls itself world #2 in assistance and travel insurance. — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/generali-reveals-redion--the-new-brand-for-the-groups-leading-global-care-platform-which-encompasses-europ-assistance-and-generali-employee-benefits-302781774.html)
- Chubb Studio's AI optimisation engine (Q1) is the most concrete AI-for-embedded-sales feature found among large carriers. — [Reinsurance News](https://www.reinsurancene.ws/chubb-unveils-new-ai-powered-embedded-insurance-optimisation-engine/)

### Inferences
- For travel insurance, AI is mostly added to the checkout through partner APIs and personalisation engines, not as a conversational sales agent. API catalogs listing AXA Partners for AI agents suggest preparation for agentic commerce, where a third-party AI travel agent buys cover, but I found no live volume data.

### Gaps
- I found no Europ Assistance, AXA Assistance or Allianz Partners source on a sales-agent copilot or a conversion metric for AI-assisted travel insurance sales.

## Q3. Which deployments publish measured outcomes, and which reported problems (hallucinations, complaints, rollbacks, regulatory pushback)?

### Takeaway
Measured outcomes are mostly about underwriting and service efficiency (Zurich, Aviva, Allianz UK, Ping An volumes). The biggest controversy on the distribution side is labour: State Farm's 2026 agent contracts mandated AI use, provoked a backlash, and were partly softened. Regulatory and legal pushback is concentrated in AI claims and underwriting decisions (GEICO/PA, UnitedHealth, Cigna, State Farm), not sales chatbots. I found no documented rollback of an insurer sales chatbot.

### Cited Findings
**Published outcomes (company-reported)**
- Zurich USMM: about 60 min saved per submission, 80% of submissions covered, 16 underwriters, more than 1M submissions processed by December 2025. — [Zurich NA](https://www.zurichna.com/media/news-releases/2025/ai-tool-brings-real-world-value-to-zurich-usmm-underwriters)
- Zurich UK: 78% cut in commercial underwriting processing time. — [Insurance Post](https://www.postonline.co.uk/technology/7960848/zurich-cuts-commercial-underwriting-time-by-78-with-ai)
- Aviva: about 50% less underwriter review time per case. — [Aviva](https://www.aviva.com/newsroom/news-releases/2025/11/aviva-to-launch-groundbreaking-ai-underwriting-tool/)
- Allianz UK BRIAN: more than 13,000 queries and an estimated 135 working days saved. — [Allianz UK](https://www.allianz.co.uk/news-and-insight/news/brian-the-ai-brain.html)
- Ping An: 80% of service volume handled by AI; AI-agent sales of about RMB140bn (2024) and RMB133bn (2025). — [Ping An 2024 deck](https://group.pingan.com/resource/pingan/IR-Docs/2025/pingan-ar24-presentation.pdf); [Ping An 2025](https://group.pingan.com/media/news/2026/ar-25.html)
- Allianz Partners US claims: lifecycle cut from 19 to 4 days. — [Travel Weekly](https://www.travelweekly.com/Travel-News/Travel-Agent-Issues/Allianz-Partners-leverages-AI-for-faster-payouts)
- An anonymous "European insurer" (McKinsey) used AI agents to personalise campaigns across hundreds of micro-segments, adapt scripts and coach sales teams in real time, with 2–3x higher conversion rates. This is a consultancy claim with the insurer unnamed. — [McKinsey](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/growth-marketing-and-sales/our-insights/agents-for-growth-turning-ai-promise-into-impact)
- Vendor claims of 4–7x conversion lifts appear in vendor marketing (Landbase and others). Treat them as unverified. — [Landbase](https://www.landbase.com/blog/best-ai-digital-sales-agents-for-gtm-strategies-2025)

**Problems, pushback and controversies**
- State Farm (May–June 2026): CEO Jon Farney told agents that new contracts after 2027 would tie pay to strict sales targets and mandate daily AI use. Secondary reports estimate income drops of up to 35–40% for some long-tenured agents (State Farm has not confirmed this). One agent called it "a real slap in the face". In late June 2026 State Farm slowed some changes: the AIPP deferred-compensation programme was extended through 2028, with sales-based metrics from 2029. It kept the end of agent insurance-benefit support on 31 December 2026. — [WGLT, 26 June 2026](https://www.wglt.org/local-news/2026-06-26/state-farm-slows-changes-for-agent-contracts); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/state-farm-overhauls-agent-contracts-115756712.html); [Insurance Business](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/breaking-news/state-farm-changes-agent-contract-terms-amid-digital-push-577247.aspx); [Insurance Business "State Farm blinked"](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/breaking-news/state-farm-blinked--that-doesnt-mean-their-agents-won-579449.aspx); [Techstrong.ai](https://techstrong.ai/articles/state-farm-agents-face-up-to-40-pay-cut-as-insurer-mandates-ai-tools-slashes-benefits/)
- Chubb: AI-linked workforce cut of about 20% signalled, which drew attention to job impacts. — [Insurance Business](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/breaking-news/chubb-ceo-signals-significant-workforce-reductions-as-ai-strategy-accelerates-568927.aspx)
- GEICO and the Pennsylvania AG: settlement over AI-driven underwriting. It started with an auto policy cancelled after an AI tool flagged the customer for extra underwriting. — [Clark Hill](https://www.clarkhill.com/news-events/news/geico-ai-settlement-insurance-underwriting-compliance/)
- Litigation over algorithmic claims decisions: the UnitedHealth nH Predict class action was partly allowed to proceed (February 2025), the Cigna PxDx class action proceeded (March 2025), and a State Farm claims-algorithm race-discrimination suit survived a motion to dismiss. These are claims cases, not sales, but they shape regulators' "human check" expectations. — [Insurance Journal](https://www.insurancejournal.com/magazines/mag-features/2025/04/21/820245.htm); [ABA, May 2026](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/dispute_resolution/resources/just-resolutions/2026-may/dispute-resolution-professionals-ai-insurance-underwriting/); [Claims Journal, June 2026](https://www.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2026/06/17/337850.htm)
- Analogous hallucination case from outside insurance: in Moffatt v. Air Canada (2024), a tribunal held the airline liable for its chatbot's invented bereavement-fare policy. This is the reference precedent for sales and service chatbot misstatements. — [The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/companies-may-be-held-liable-when-ai-harms-their-clients-and-customers-282234)
- Lloyd's market insurers (Armilla) launched cover for losses caused by AI chatbot errors, which suggests chatbot-error risk is now treated as insurable. — [Armilla](https://www.armilla.ai/resources/insurers-launch-cover-for-losses-caused-by-ai-chatbot-errors)
- UK FCA asked AI firms to warn users about the limits of chatbot financial advice. The source says only 40% of consumers understand they have no recourse. — [Finextra](https://finextra.com/newsarticle/48604/fca-calls-for-disclaimers-on-ai-financial-advice)

### Inferences
- Publicly reported failures are about labour and fairness, not technical hallucinations in sales flows. Insurers likely avoid disclosing those, and the conservative suggest-only design limits their exposure.
- Ping An's AI-agent sales fell slightly from 2024 to 2025 (about 140bn to 133bn RMB), so its AI-sales channel may not be growing. Its definitions are unclear, though.

### Gaps
- I found no documented case of a large insurer withdrawing a sales or agent copilot over hallucinations or complaints.
- I found no published error-rate metrics for any AI quoting or advice tool.

## Q4. What governance patterns do these companies describe (human-in-the-loop, AI ethics boards, explainability)?

### Takeaway
The pattern is consistent: AI assists and a human decides. Underwriters review AI summaries (Zurich, Aviva, Allianz BRIAN), AI recommends and humans approve (Allianz Partners claims), and complex cases go to human advisors (Chubb click-to-engage, Progressive "knee-to-knee"). AI runs in secured enterprise environments (AXA Secure GPT on Azure). This sits under regulatory frameworks such as the NAIC model bulletin and EIOPA monitoring.

### Cited Findings
- AXA Secure GPT runs in a "secured and data-privacy compliant" cloud environment rather than a public consumer tool. — [AXA](https://www.axa.com/en/press/press-releases/axa-offers-securegenerative-ai-to-employees)
- Zurich publishes an "AI at Zurich" page on its responsible-AI approach. Its underwriting tools are positioned as assistants to underwriters. — [Zurich](https://www.zurich.com/about-us/ai-at-zurich)
- Allianz Partners: AI "reviews and recommends" claims for approval, which keeps a human approval step. — [Travel Weekly](https://www.travelweekly.com/Travel-News/Travel-Agent-Issues/Allianz-Partners-leverages-AI-for-faster-payouts)
- Chubb Studio routes customers from AI recommendations to human advisors by phone, video or text. — [Reinsurance News](https://www.reinsurancene.ws/chubb-unveils-new-ai-powered-embedded-insurance-optimisation-engine/)
- State Farm frames its approach publicly as "Human + Digital", combining technology with personal service. — [Carrier Management](https://www.carriermanagement.com/news/2026/05/12/287814.htm)
- The NAIC model AI bulletin (December 2023) had been acted on by 23 states plus DC as of 5 August 2025. — [ABA](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/dispute_resolution/resources/just-resolutions/2026-may/dispute-resolution-professionals-ai-insurance-underwriting/); [Kansas Legislative Research Briefing Book 2026](https://klrd.gov/2026/03/02/briefing-book-2026-artificial-intelligence-use-in-health-insurance/)
- EIOPA's gen-AI survey (report August 2025, 347 undertakings in 25 countries) found widespread and growing gen-AI adoption in European insurance. — [EIOPA](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/eiopa-surveys-european-insurers-their-use-generative-ai-2025-05-15_en); [DataGuidance](https://www.dataguidance.com/news/eu-eiopa-publishes-report-use-generative-ai-europes)
- Regulators increasingly expect a human check on AI-driven adverse decisions. — [Talkory](https://www.talkory.ai/blog/ai-in-insurance-claims-regulators-want-a-human-check) (low-reliability blog); [Insurance Journal](https://www.insurancejournal.com/magazines/mag-features/2025/04/21/820245.htm)

### Inferences
- For quote-to-bind, the emerging line is to let AI act autonomously on simple, high-volume, low-severity transactions (Allstate personal lines, Allianz Nemo under AUD 500). Commercial and life underwriting and complex advice stay human-decided, with AI preparing the file.

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve details on named ethics boards or explainability tooling (for example Allianz's or AXA's AI governance frameworks, or ERGO and Munich Re). These need full-text reads of the annual reports, which failed in this session.
